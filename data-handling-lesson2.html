Handling and Modeling Data

ICT / Data Management Lesson

Lesson Overview

Lesson Objectives:

  • Carry out a full investigation by gathering peer data.
  • Organize raw data into structured tables.
  • Input data and decide on the best display methods.
  • Plan visual formatting (headers/colors) for a data model.

Resources Needed:

  • Data capture forms (from previous lesson).
  • Computers/Tablets with spreadsheet software.
  • Design and planning area.

Total Duration: 80 Minutes

25:00

1. The Investigation Phase

This is where we gather our "Raw Data".

  • Self-Completion: Complete your own form first.
  • Data Collection: Pass your forms around the room. Ensure everyone in class responds to your form.
  • Quality Check: Once you get your form back, verify every classmate has provided a response.

Checklist:

I have completed my own form.
Every classmate has signed/answered my form.
I have verified the quality of the data (no missing cells).
15:00

2. Organizing and Categorizing

Raw data is messy. Let's turn it into a Concise Table.

Review your results (e.g., a list of names under colors). Working with a partner, define your headers.

Drafting your Table Structure:

Header 1 (e.g. Category) Header 2 (e.g. Total Count)

4. Digital Entry and Implementation

Now, move to your device and start the Modeling.

  • Data Entry: Type your organized table into the spreadsheet.
  • Modeling: Apply the colors, bold headers, and insert the chart you selected in the previous step.
