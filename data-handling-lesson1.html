Handling and Modelling Data

ICT / Data Literacy Intro

15 Minutes

1. Introduction to Research

How well do you know your classmates? Data handling starts with asking questions!

Key Term: Respondent

Definition: A person who replies to a questionnaire or data capture form.

Discussion Activity

Think about something you want to discover about the people in this room. Is it their favorite sport? Their preferred snacks?

Goal: Today we will design an investigation to collect and handle this data accurately.

15 Minutes

2. Case Study: The "Colours" Investigation

Let's look at a successful example.

Topic: Favourite Colours

The Statement: "My favourite colour is..."

Data Items (The Choices):

  1. Red
  2. Blue
  3. Green
  4. Yellow
  5. Pink
  6. Purple
  7. Orange
  8. Black
  9. White
  10. Brown

Why exactly 10 items? This keeps our data manageable and consistent for the whole class!

20 Minutes

3. Designing Your Investigation

Work with a partner to choose your focus. Select exactly 10 items.

Item Selection (List 10)

Crucial Instruction: "Choose one item only." Why is this important? (Discuss with your partner).

20 Minutes

4. Construct Your Data Capture Form

Now, build the actual form that your Respondents will fill out.

Check for spelling mistakes! Is the form easy to read?

10 Minutes

5. Reflection & Verification

Final Checklist:

  • Is my topic clear?
  • Do I have exactly 10 data items?
  • Is there a clear statement for the respondent?
  • Are there instructions?
